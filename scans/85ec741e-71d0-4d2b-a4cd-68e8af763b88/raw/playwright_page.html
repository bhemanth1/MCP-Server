We Give U an Edge.

As a global university, Woxsen aims to empower its students with a global mind-set through a cutting-edge curriculum, world-class pedagogy, industry connections and international partnerships.

Academic Excellence

We are committed to creating a transformative learning experience for our students through our adaptability, resilience and commitment to excellence in all its manifestations.

Applied Learning

We take a discovery-based approach to learning in all our programmes. A practical process that seeks to promote innovation, student motivation and real-life training. 

Global Outlook

We have collaborated with the world’s leading universities for curriculum development, student mobility, research and training to help imbue in our students a global perspective

Diversity & Inclusivity

Our commitment to diversity, inclusivity and pluralism is core to founding values and is deeply embedded in all our governance, collaborations and other key functions.

Robust Industry Connect

Curriculum for all our programmes is developed in close coordination with industry leaders to ensure that students gain the requisite knowledge and skills for an ever-changing job market.

Sports Arena

Woxsen University has taken its students' growth and development to a whole new level by creating an international standard sports arena called ‘The League’ which boasts state-of-the-art infrastructure and sporting facilities

World-class Infrastructure

The 200 acre world-class, verdant university campus inspires creative learning amidst state-of-the-art labs, hi-tech classrooms, recreation and residential facilities.

We Take U Far. Very Far.

Since its inception in 2014, Woxsen University has established over 170+ partnerships with the world’s leading universities and institutions across 50+ countries. These collaborations help our students and faculty pursue a wide range of international opportunities including student exchange, progressive studies along with teaching and research opportunities.
